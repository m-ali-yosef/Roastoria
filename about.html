Our Purpose & Heritage

Crafted for the Real Coffee Enthusiast.

Roastoria was established with a singular mission: to make freshly roasted specialty coffee transparent, accessible, and uncomplicated across Egypt. We roast in artisan micro-batches in Cairo and dispatch immediately so peak aromatics never fade.

☕

100% Arabica 84+ SCA

Every coffee lot is scored for clarity, natural sweetness, and clean cup acidity before sourcing.

⏳

48h Roast-to-Ship

We believe supermarket coffee is dead coffee. You always receive beans roasted at their peak flavor window.

🤝

Ethical Direct Trade

Direct farm partnerships in Colombia, Brazil, and Ethiopia that pay farmers sustainable specialty premiums.

Specialty Coffee Guide

Understanding Specialty Coffee Flavor Notes

When we say coffee has notes of 'Bergamot, Red Apple, or Dark Chocolate', we never add synthetic syrup or flavor drops. These natural profiles develop through terroir, elevation, cherry fermentation, and micro-batch roasting precision.

Brewing extraordinary coffee at home requires balancing water temperature (92°C–96°C), brew ratio (1:15 to 1:16), and grind particle uniformity to ensure sweet, aromatic extraction.
